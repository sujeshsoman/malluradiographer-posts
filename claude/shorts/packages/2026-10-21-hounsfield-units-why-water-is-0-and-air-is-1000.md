**Fact-check list**

- [ ] Water (distilled, at STP) is 0 HU and air is -1000 HU; HU come from a linear transformation of measured attenuation coefficients. REF_1
- [ ] Named after Sir Godfrey Hounsfield, Nobel prize winner and inventor of CT. REF_1
- [ ] Typical values: fat around -50 HU, CSF around +15 HU, blood +30 to +45 HU. REF_2
- [ ] Most tissues sit between -100 and +100 HU; very dense bone (cochlea) around 2000 HU; metals over 3000 HU. REF_1
- [ ] HU is a relative scale; beam energy (kV), reconstruction algorithm and CT design change it; beam hardening can lower HU; positive values display brighter, negative darker. REF_2

REF_1: [Hounsfield unit (Radiopaedia)](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/hounsfield-unit) · REF_2: [Hounsfield Unit - StatPearls (NCBI Bookshelf)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK547721/)
